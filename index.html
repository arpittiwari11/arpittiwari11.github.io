About Me

Who I Am

Hello! I’m Arpit

I started out as a Mechanical Engineer at IIT Bombay, but somewhere along the way, a dual degree in AI & Data Science pulled me into a completely different world: building systems that can make sense of messy, real-world problems.

At Meesho, I found one of the messiest problems there is: the Indian address.

“Near the temple, behind the blue gate.”
It means almost nothing to a map, yet a parcel still has to find its way there.

That challenge pulled me deeper into AI and geospatial intelligence, and led me to build systems that make sense of addresses at scale. GeoIndia is a generative geocoder that turns multilingual, unstructured addresses into precise locations, while Spatial Brain builds a geographic memory entirely from delivery data. I’ve also built optimisation and pricing systems that help make Meesho’s logistics network more efficient and reduce the cost of delivering every order.

Today, I lead the Address Enrichment & Prediction charter, working at the intersection of AI, geospatial intelligence, and logistics. My work has been published at CIKM, AAAI, and SIGSPATIAL, and I mentor data scientists across logistics and geo infrastructure.

Away from work, you’ll usually find me exploring new technology, travelling, or on a cricket field.

UAID: an address-to-location memory

Gives every address a unique ID and learns its true location from past deliveries. Differently typed versions of the same address collapse into one record; a location is served only when past deliveries agree.

  • 300M+ UAIDs, with 6M+ refreshed every day.
